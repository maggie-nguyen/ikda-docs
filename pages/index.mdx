## Break the Intermediate Plateau

Most Korean learners get stuck. Grammar books only go so far. Ikda gets you reading **real Korean** — news articles, books, and stories — with every word a tap away.

## Who is Ikda for?

| Level | Who | What Ikda does |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| <span className="level-beginner">Beginner</span> | Knows Hangul, basic phrases (TOPIK 1–2) | Build reading habits with simple content |
| <span className="level-intermediate">Intermediate</span> | Holds conversations, reads simple texts (TOPIK 3–4) | Push through the plateau with real content |
| <span className="level-advanced">Advanced</span> | Reads news comfortably (TOPIK 5–6) | Refine nuance with native-level material |

Ikda is especially designed for **intermediate learners** — the stage where most people plateau and give up. Reading real Korean every day is the fastest path forward.
