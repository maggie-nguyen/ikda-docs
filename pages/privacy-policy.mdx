## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you register, we collect:
- Email address
- First and last name (optional)
- Password (stored hashed — never in plain text)

### Profile & Learning Data
- Korean proficiency level
- Daily study goal (minutes)
- Study streak and last active date
- Timezone and notification preferences

### Usage Data
- Vocabulary words saved and review history
- Books and news articles read
- TOPIK practice results
- Word translations accessed

### Device & Technical Data
- FCM token (for push notifications, if enabled)
- Device type and OS version (standard app analytics)

### Payment Data
Subscription status (premium/free) and in-app purchase events via RevenueCat. We **do not** store full payment card details. Payments are processed by Apple App Store, Google Play, or RevenueCat on your behalf.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

| Purpose | Data used |
|---------|-----------|
| Provide the app and its features | Account, learning, usage data |
| Personalize content to your level | Korean level, usage history |
| Send study reminders | FCM token, notification time |
| Process subscriptions | RevenueCat events, premium status |
| Improve the app | Aggregated usage analytics |
| Respond to support requests | Email, account info |

## 3. Data Sharing

We **do not sell** your personal data. We share data only with:
- **RevenueCat** — subscription and purchase management
- **Firebase / Google** — push notification delivery (FCM)
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers** — to operate our backend

All processors are bound by data processing agreements.

## 4. Data Retention

- Active accounts: retained while your account exists
- Deleted accounts: data removed within 30 days
- Anonymized analytics: may be retained indefinitely

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and associated data
- Export your data
- Withdraw consent for notifications at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at **everpinestudio@gmail.com**.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Ikda is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, and access controls. No system is 100% secure — we encourage strong, unique passwords.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
