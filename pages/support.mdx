## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Cancel through your **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android) account settings. Your premium access continues until the end of the current billing period.

### How do I delete my account?
Email [everpinestudio@gmail.com](mailto:everpinestudio@gmail.com) with your registered email and subject line **"Delete my account"**. We will remove your data within 30 days.

### I forgot my password.
Use the **"Forgot password"** link on the login screen. A reset email will be sent to your registered address.

### The app is not loading content.
- Check your internet connection
- Force-close and reopen the app
- If the problem persists, contact us with your device model and OS version

### My streak reset incorrectly.
Contact us with your email and the date(s) affected. We review streak corrections case by case.

### What is the difference between Free and Premium?
Free gives access to core reading and vocab features. Premium unlocks the full content library, all TOPIK practice sets, and advanced learning features. Upgrade anytime inside the app.
